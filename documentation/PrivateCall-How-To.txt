PRIVATECALL - HOW TO MAKE A CALL
================================

No account, installation, terminal, or programming knowledge is needed.
Both participants simply open the HTML file.

BEFORE YOU START
- Both of you need the file PrivateCall.html and an internet connection.
- Open it in Google Chrome. (Chrome is the browser PrivateCall was
  tested with.)
- You also need another way to send each other a message, such as a
  chat app or e-mail.
- To find a connection, PrivateCall contacts public helper servers run by
  Google and Cloudflare. Some networks also need a relay server (see
  "Troubleshooting").


PERSON A - STARTS THE CALL
1. Open PrivateCall.html and click "Start a call".
2. Choose "Video call" or "Voice call". When the browser asks, allow the
   microphone (and the camera).
3. Wait a moment. On some networks this takes up to 30 seconds.
4. Under "Send this invitation", click "Copy". Send the whole message to
   Person B in any app. (Or click "Save as file" and send the file.)
5. Keep the window open. When Person B's reply arrives, copy all of it.
   Under "Add their reply", click "Paste". You can also paste into the
   box, or click "Open file" for a reply file.
   The call starts by itself.


PERSON B - JOINS THE CALL
1. Open PrivateCall.html and click "Join a call".
2. Copy the whole invitation you received. Under "Add the invitation",
   click "Paste". You can also paste into the box, or click "Open file".
3. Click "Join with camera" or "Join with microphone only".
   (For a voice call the button is "Join the voice call".)
   When the browser asks, allow the microphone (and the camera).
4. Under "Send this reply back", click "Copy" and send the whole reply
   to Person A.
5. Wait. The screen says: "Waiting for them to add your reply. The call
   starts automatically."


COPY THE WHOLE MESSAGE
Each message starts with a sentence, for example:
   Join my private call: open PrivateCall, choose "Join a call",
   and paste this whole message.
Then comes the line "PrivateCall.invite.v1:" (or "PrivateCall.reply.v1:"),
lines of letters and numbers, and finally ":end".
Copy and send everything, from the first sentence to ":end".
Do not change anything. An invitation can be used for one call only.


CHECK THE SECURITY CODE (BOTH OF YOU)
When the call connects, both screens show the same 6-digit code, for
example:  482 917
Until you click "Codes match", the other person can't see you. They can
hear you only while your microphone is turned on in this code panel.

- Person A clicks "Turn on microphone" and says the FIRST three digits:
  "four eight two".
- Person B clicks "Turn on microphone" and says the LAST three digits:
  "nine one seven".
- Each of you compares what you hear with your own screen.
- Only if everything matches, click "Codes match". Each of you clicks it
  on your own screen. Your camera and microphone are sent normally only
  after you do.

If the codes are different: click "Codes don't match". The call ends.
Do not continue. Start a new call, and send the invitation through a
different app if you can.

A stronger check: a skilled attacker could imitate a voice. For more
certainty, compare the code as text in a different app you trust.
Click "Copy code" and send it there. Both codes must be the same.


DURING THE CALL
- "Microphone" - turn your microphone off or on (or press M).
- "Camera"     - turn your camera off or on (or press V).
- "Leave"      - end the call.
- "Details"    - information about the connection.
- If you see "Click to turn on sound", click it to hear the other person.


TROUBLESHOOTING
Permission denied
  The message says PrivateCall wasn't allowed to use your microphone or
  camera. Click the icon in the browser's address bar, allow access,
  then try again.

No sound
  Click "Click to turn on sound" if you see it. Check your speaker or
  headset volume. Before the other person clicks "Codes match", you
  normally hear them only while their microphone is turned on in the
  code panel.

Invitation or reply not accepted
  Copy the whole message again, from the first sentence to ":end", or
  send it as a file ("Save as file" and "Open file"). The invitation
  goes into "Join a call". The reply goes back to the computer that
  started the call. A used invitation does not work again; ask for a
  new one.

"Couldn't connect"
  Start again and exchange a new invitation and reply quickly, within a
  few minutes. Try a different network for one of you, such as a phone
  hotspot or home Wi-Fi.

When a relay may be needed
  If calls keep failing, especially on office, school, mobile or other
  restricted networks, you may need a relay server. Someone you trust
  who runs one can give you a relay code that starts with "PCRELAY1:".
  Open "Connection settings", paste the code, and click "Use this relay".
  Only one of you needs it.
