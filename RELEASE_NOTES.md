PrivateCall 1.2.0 — portable, single-file WebRTC voice and video calls.

- Open `PrivateCall.html` directly from disk in Google Chrome; no account or installer.
- Connect through manually exchanged invitations and replies.
- Compare the six-digit security code before ordinary microphone/camera transmission.
- Minimal interface, optional TURN relay configuration, and connection diagnostics.

**Security notice:** Experimental software. Not independently audited or validated on restrictive networks. Do not rely on it for high-risk communications.

Download `PrivateCall.html` below. Use `SHA256SUMS` to check its SHA-256 digest. See `PrivateCall-How-To.pdf` for instructions.
